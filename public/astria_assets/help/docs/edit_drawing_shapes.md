#### Edit drawing shapes

**Select a shape**<br>
Click on the select tool in the drawing tab under drawing tools. Then click on a shape you have drawn on the image. To select multiple shapes, hold shift and click on the shapes you want to select.

**Edit a shape**<br>
Controls for your selected shape(s) will appear in the right side panel. Different shapes have different customizable properties. To move your selected shape(s), click and drag. To resize a shape, click and drag on the shape handles that appear on your selected shape. Only one shape can be resized at a time. Double click on a line, polyline, arrow, or polygon to edit the individual points of the shape. To delete a point of a polyline or polygon, click on one of the points and press the `delete` key.

**Delete a shape**<br>
To delete your selected shape(s), press the `delete` key or click on the `Remove Object` button in the right side panel.
