#### Measure distances

The {APPNAME} measurement tool allows you to measure the distance between two XYZ points. You can create as many measurements on an image as you would like.

**Create a measurement**<br>
To start measuring, click the `measure` button in the top toolbar or use the keyboard shortcut `m`. Click on the image to place the first point of your measurement. As you move your cursor around the image, you will see a dotted line from your first point to your cursor. If you stop moving your cursor briefly, it will start calculating the distance between the two points. Click again to place the second point and finish the measurement.

Hold down the `option` (Mac) or `alt` (Windows) key to pan within the image without placing a measurement end point.

**Edit a measurement**<br>
To begin editing a measurement you have already made, click on it. You can then edit the measurement by either clicking and dragging one of the points or clicking and dragging the dotted line to move the whole measurement. To finish editing your measurement click somewhere else on the image or hit escape.

**Delete a measurement**<br>
To delete a single measurement, click to begin editing the measurement and then press the `delete` key. To delete all measurements, click on the arrow on the right side of the `Measure` button in the top application toolbar and then press `Remove All`.

**Additional information**<br>
If the measure button is grayed out, the product you are viewing does not have a corresponding XYZ overlay which is required for measurement computation. If you are able to measure but the measurement says `unknown` it means that no XYZ data is available for at least one of the measurement endpoints. To see where the XYZ data is, try turning on the XYZ overlay in the overlays panel.

The best way to save or share measurements you have made is to bookmark or share your current url.
