#### Access data values

Select the `Values` tab on the right side panel. Here you will see a list of data products compatible with your base image. If you want to query other products, you must change your base image.

`ctrl` + `click` on a pixel to get data values for that pixel. Under the subheader `Cursor Values` you will see the line and sample of the cursor you placed. To see the line/sample of your mouse as you move around the image, look at the bottom toolbar below the image. For cylindrical mosaics, you will also see azimuth and elevation in these same locations.

The products that are queried when you place a cursor are controlled by the options in the `Available Products` subheader. By default, your base image will be selected (shown as green). To add or remove products, click on the products here and you will see them appear under `Cursor Values` if a cursor is placed. By default, any RDR overlays added in the `RDRs` tab will also be added here.
