#### Annotate an image

Select the `Draw` tab on the right side panel and click `New Drawing`. Alternatively, you can just click the `Draw` button on the top toolbar. The right side panel will now be covered by the drawing panel. Click on any of the drawing tools to start annotating the image. Once you have drawn a shape on the image, controls will appear to change the stroke, opacity, and other properties of the shape. In order to save your drawing, give it a title and optionally a description then click `Save Drawing`. Your drawing will now be searchable, shareable, and viewable by other {APPNAME} users.

**Export High Resolution Copy of a Drawing**<br>
A drawing is defined by a file in OCS that describes all of the objects the user has created. A high resolution image copy of the drawing can be automatically created during the drawing saving process if desired for easy bulk access and archiving of drawings. The drawing will export at the resolution of the base image up to a width of 4320 pixels, after which the image will be downsampled to avoid potentially slow exports. The exports are saved as JPGs with the same name as the drawing title. If you wish to create a one-off export of drawings with more control over the resolution or with additional content such as targets, measurements, etc., you should make use of the general {APPNAME} export functionality in the top navbar.
