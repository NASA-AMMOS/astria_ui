#### Mark Features in an Image

Marking features is a way to annotate parts of an image with Science Intent keywords.

You can label features, indicate your level of confidence in your marking, and share features with others.

To begin, select the `Features` tab in the right side of the application. You will see any features that have been created for the currently selected image with options to show/hide them. There will also be a button labeled `Add Feature`, click this to open the `Image Feature Editor`.

Within the `Image Feature Editor` you can select the type of feature you would like to mark from the dropdown. You can type in this field to quickly search through the list. If you don't see the feature you are interested in, click the `Request New Keyword` button to start an email to the Science Information Manager to request a new keyword.

To begin marking a specific area in the image, click the `Mark Area on Image` button or press 'f'. Then you can draw a polygon around the area of interest by left-clicking at various points in the image and double-clicking to complete your shape. You can mark multiple areas in the image with the same feature type and when you hit `Save` each area will become its own feature listing. This allows for granular feature tagging and editing within an image.

After you've marked your area(s) you can select your desired confidence level. This will indicate to you and others how sure you are of the given feature tag.

You can then optionally add notes to your marking(s) and then press `Save Feature` to save your marked features to OCS. All marked areas will share the same feature, confidence level, and notes but can be edited individually after being saved.
