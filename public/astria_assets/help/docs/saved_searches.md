#### Saved searches

<b>Saving your Current Search</b><br>
Select the `Saved Searches` button on the top toolbar. Name your search in the input provided and click save. This will save all of the search filters you have selected in both the `Sol` and `Search` tabs. Your saved searches are only viewable by you. They will be stored on OCS and available to load whenever you are using {APPNAME}.

<b>Loading a Saved Search</b><br>
Select a search in the `Saved Searches` dropdown. This will remove any currently active search filters in both `Sol` and `Image` tabs and will add the filters stored in the saved search

<b>Modifying a Saved Search</b><br>
Click on the edit icon next to the name of your search in the `Saved Searches` dropdown. This will bring up an edit window where you have the option to rename your search, overwrite it with your current search, or delete it.

<b>Sharing a Saved Search</b><br>
The best way to share your saved search with someone is to turn on your saved search, copy the current {APPNAME} url, and send it to them. When they open this link, all of the same search filters will be applied and they can create a new saved search for themselves with these filters.
